The Gospel

I don't just believe in God — I follow Him. Here's what that means, and why I think it's the most important thing I can share with you.

Who is Jesus?

Jesus is the Son of God, the perfect sacrifice, the one who bore the sin of every person who has ever lived. He is God in human flesh — facing the same temptations any of us face, fully man and fully God at once.

Why do we need Him?

Every single person has fallen short of the glory of God. A single white lie, something taken that wasn't ours, even lust in our eyes — any of it separates us from God eternally.

We push God aside and say "I'll do it my way," and we get it wrong every single time. That's exactly why we need Jesus: because we can't do it ourselves. It's our nature.

What did He do?

Jesus, being perfect and holy, outweighed all the sin of the world so that you could be in heaven with Him. What He did was a free gift, given because He wants you there. Anyone can receive it.

But it wouldn't mean much if He were still dead. Good news — He's alive. Death couldn't hold Him, and He rose three days later.

How do I receive it?

You might think you have to earn God's grace. You can't. It is impossible to enter the kingdom of heaven on our own strength.

It isn't about what you have or haven't done. It's about what Jesus did for you on the cross. It's purely a gift from God — you cannot earn it, but His love knows no bounds, so He gives it freely.

What comes after forgiveness?

Jesus said, "If you love me, keep my commands." Because we accept God into our hearts, we love Him — because He loved us first. And when you receive that love, the thing to do with it is give it to others, and share this with everyone.

"We love because he first loved us." 1 John 4:19 (NIV)

What about heaven?

Suppose you were standing before God right now and He asked you, "Why should I let you into heaven?" What would you say?

What you need to know is that God loves you, and He has made a way for you to know for certain where you will spend eternity.

"For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life." John 3:16

Whoever believes. That includes you.

The Sinner's Prayer

"Lord Jesus, I know I am a sinner and I cannot save myself. I believe You died on the cross for my sins and rose again. I turn from my sin and I invite You into my heart and life as my Lord and Savior. Thank You for forgiving me and giving me eternal life. In Jesus' name, Amen."
