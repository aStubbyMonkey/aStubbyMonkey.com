The Gospel

I don't just believe in God — I follow Him. Here's what that means, and why I think it's the most important thing I can share with you.

✝️

Who is Jesus?

Jesus is the Son of God, who is the Perfect Sacrifice, the one who bore all of the sin of every man who ever lived on this planet. He is God in Human Flesh, who has the temptations of a normal person, while being fully man and fully God.

💔

Why do we need Him?

Every Single Person has fallen short of the Glory of God; just a single White Lie, Stolen Object, or even Lust in our eyes, will separate us from God eternally. We essentially push God aside and say, "I'mma do it my way," and we screw it up every. single. time. That's why we need Jesus, because we can't do it; it's our Human Nature.

🕊️

What did He do?

Jesus being all Perfect & Holy outweighed all of the sin of the world, so that you could be in heaven with Him. What Jesus did was a free gift, all because he wants you in Heaven. Anyone can acquire this gift. But it doesn't matter if he's dead, right? Well, good news — he's alive. Death couldn't hold him, and he rose just 3 days later from the grave.

🙌

How do I receive it?

Do you think we can earn God's Grace? Because we can't, it IS impossible for us to enter into the kingdom of heaven on our own strength. It's not about what you have or haven't done. It's about what Jesus did for you on the rugged cross. This is Purely a Gift from God. You cannot earn it, but God's love knows no bounds, so he'll give it to you freely.

📖

What's After Forgiveness?

Jesus said "If you love me, keep my commands" John 14:15 NIV, and because we accept God into our hearts we should Love him because Jesus first loved us. So when you get the Love of the lord, we should take that love and give it to others, sharing the Gospel to everyone because this is how we should live our lives here on Earth. "We love because he first loved us" 1 John 4:19 NIV

❤️

Heaven after Death?

Suppose you were standing before God right now and He asked you, "Why should I let you into heaven?" What would you say? What you need to know is that God loves you and has provided a way that you can know for sure where you will spend eternity. The Bible states it this way: "For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life" John 3:16. So if you believe, that means it includes you. God's Love know no bounds.

✝

The Sinner's Prayer

"Lord Jesus, I know I am a sinner and I cannot save myself. I believe You died on the cross for my sins and rose again. I turn from my sin and I invite You into my heart and life as my Lord and Savior. Thank You for forgiving me and giving me eternal life. In Jesus' name, Amen."

If you prayed that and meant it — welcome to the family. 🙏
Reach out on Discord. I'd love to talk with you.
