The Gospel

I don't just believe in God — I follow Him. Here's what that means, and why I think it's the most important thing I can share with you.

✝️

Who is Jesus?

Jesus is the Son of God — fully God and fully human. He lived a perfect life, was crucified, died, and rose from the dead. He is alive today.

John 14:6 — "I am the way, the truth, and the life."
💔

Why do we need Him?

Every person has sinned and fallen short of God's standard. The consequence is separation from God — but God loved us too much to leave us there.

Romans 3:23 — "All have sinned and fall short of the glory of God."
🕊️

What did He do?

Jesus took the punishment we deserved. His death paid the full price for our sin. His resurrection proved He had power over death — and offers that same life to us.

Romans 5:8 — "While we were still sinners, Christ died for us."
🙌

How do I receive it?

Believe that Jesus is Lord, that He died for your sins, and that God raised Him. Confess it. Turn from sin. Salvation is a gift — not something earned.

Romans 10:9 — "If you confess... and believe... you will be saved."
📖

I'm still learning too

I don't have all the answers — and I'm not afraid to say that. But I'm not afraid of any question. If you want to talk faith or Christianity, hit me up.

1 Peter 3:15 — "Always be prepared to give an answer... with gentleness."
❤️

God's love is for you

This isn't religion — it's a relationship. God knows you by name. He's not waiting for you to be good enough. He's been pursuing you this whole time.

John 3:16 — "For God so loved the world that He gave His only Son."
✝

The Sinner's Prayer

"Lord Jesus, I know I am a sinner and I cannot save myself. I believe You died on the cross for my sins and rose again. I turn from my sin and I invite You into my heart and life as my Lord and Savior. Thank You for forgiving me and giving me eternal life. In Jesus' name, Amen."

If you prayed that and meant it — welcome to the family. 🙏
Reach out on Discord. I'd love to talk with you.
